Case 04 · Performance Growth

Always-on demand built to learn, optimize and scale.

Performance marketing works best when media, lifecycle operations and funnel measurement are connected. I owned paid search strategy while strengthening the systems that moved demand toward Sales readiness.

Paid searchLifecycleMarketoHubSpotSalesforceReporting
The operating idea

Traffic is useful only if it becomes qualified demand.

The program connected always-on media optimization with lead scoring, routing, nurture, targeted audiences and reporting so performance could be judged farther down the funnel.

01

Own the always-on engine

Managed strategy and execution of paid search as a continuous demand program rather than a series of isolated flights.

02

Test + optimize continuously

Used ongoing testing and performance analysis to improve traffic quality, qualified lead volume and return on investment.

03

Strengthen funnel operations

Built and optimized lead scoring, routing, qualification and nurture workflows in Marketo and HubSpot.

04

Target the right accounts

Partnered with Sales and ISR teams to build focused ABM audiences that improved Lead-to-MQL conversion.

05

Measure for decisions

Built reports and dashboards across Salesforce, marketing platforms and analytics tools to keep optimization tied to business outcomes.

Performance that moved beyond clicks.

The outcomes came from continuous optimization paired with stronger funnel operations and targeted audiences.
