Case 02 · Growth / Reactivation

Finding growth where others might stop looking.

Historical inactivity did not automatically mean low future propensity. Renewed intent + engagement + account fit could reveal accounts returning to market.

Dormant accountsIntentEngagementAccount historySales follow-up
The hypothesis

Growth doesn’t always mean finding a new audience.

Instead of treating low historical engagement as permanent, I looked for return-to-market behavior and layered it with account fit, history and journey information.

Why this matters

Signal changes can reopen closed assumptions.

A dormant segment is not necessarily a dead segment. The useful question is whether new market behavior changes what the account deserves next.

Observe

What changed?

Look for renewed engagement, intent or journey movement.

Contextualize

Does it matter?

Layer fit, relationship, account history and past activity.

Prioritize

Who deserves action?

Rank the strongest return-to-market signals instead of treating all dormant accounts equally.

Activate

What should happen next?

Coordinate nurture, advertising and Sales follow-up around the new signal.
