How the motion is built

Context first. Channels second.

Audience

Who matters?

Account segment, industry, potential, relationship and relevant personas where known.

Message

Why now?

Translate the industry use case into a concrete buying context and industry-specific value proposition.

Signals

What changed?

Use engagement, intent, website behavior and journey movement to adjust treatment and determine where Sales action is warranted.

Measurement

What happened next?

Track follow-up, account progression, opportunity, pipeline and leading indicators.
